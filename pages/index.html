Two rules, chaos, then a highway after about 10,000 steps. The ant is a one-hot mask and a direction: looking, flipping and moving are whole-array operations, and the same code would move a thousand ants.

N-body gravity

Gravity between every pair of bodies at once, with no loops: the pairs' displacements form a cube, the cube gives every pair's pull, and one reduce sums them into accelerations. A figure-eight three-body orbit, a binary star with planets, a collapsing cluster, Kepler's ellipse.
