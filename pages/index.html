Demos

Small programs in X_eTaL, a typed array language, that make something worth watching. Each one shows its program beside the result, so you can see a whole loop nest happen as one array expression.

Life microscope

Conway's Life in one line of X_eTaL, with every array the line builds drawn: the nine shifted boards, their sum, the two masks, the next board. Click a cell to see its arithmetic.

Julia sets

One X_eTaL function gives the Mandelbrot set (c the grid) and every Julia set (c one number, extended over the grid). Pick c on the Mandelbrot map, or play c around its edge and watch the Julia set morph.

Wave tank

The wave equation on a grid: waves pass through a double slit and interfere, bend through a lens of slow water, and ripple where you click. Walls, sources and lenses are masks; each step is a stencil over the whole grid.

Cellular automata lab

Every rule is a lookup table: rotations turn each cell's neighbourhood into a number and the number picks its next state. Wolfram's Rule 30, 90 and 110 with an editable 8-entry table; Life, Brian's Brain and Wireworld as editable state-by-neighbours tables.
