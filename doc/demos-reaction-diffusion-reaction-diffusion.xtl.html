programdemos/reaction-diffusion/reaction-diffusion.xtl

Reaction-diffusion (Gray-Scott): two chemicals U and V on a grid. U is fed in, V eats U to make more V (U + 2V -> 3V), V is removed, and both spread to their neighbors. Every step is a few array expressions over the whole grid: four shifted copies for the spreading, products for the reaction. The patterns (spots, mazes, coral) appear by themselves. Run it with "xetal run demos/reaction-diffusion/reaction-diffusion.xtl"; it prints the seeded grid's shape, a maze drawn with characters, and V's total (grown from the seed's 9.0). The live page runs it frame by frame and lets you click a cell for its neighbors and its update's arithmetic: https://softwarewrighter.github.io/X_eTaL-demos/reaction-diffusion/

u:s_tep s: the state s is two planes, U and V. Each spreads by its own rate times its Laplacian; u * v * v is where V meets U and turns U into V (the reaction); U is fed at rate f, V removed at rate f + k.

  u ← 1 s̲elect s
  v ← 2 s̲elect s
  uvv ← u × v × v
