programdemos/wave-tank/wave-tank.xtl

A wave tank: the wave equation on a grid. Each step, every cell moves towards the average of its neighbors, carrying momentum from the step before: next = 2 u - previous + c^2 * (neighbors - 4 u). Walls are a mask, a source shakes up and down, and waves spread, bend through slits and interfere, all as array expressions over the whole grid. Run it with "xetal run demos/wave-tank/wave-tank.xtl"; it prints the state's shape and the surface drawn with characters (crests, troughs, the wall). The live page picks the scene (a double slit, a lens, two sources, an open tank) and lets you click for ripples: https://softwarewrighter.github.io/X_eTaL-demos/wave-tank/

ᵘs̲tep : (Float, Float, Float) -> (Float, Float, Float)

function · line 63

u:s_tep (u, p, t): the state is a tuple, the surface now, the surface a step ago, and the time, taken apart by the pattern and given back as a new tuple (D7, landed); t is a plain scalar now, not a plane carrying the same number everywhere, so the source's drive reads it directly. Walls hold the surface at 0 (damp * wall both zero there).

ᵘs̲tep ← { (u, p, t) →
  drive ← amp × src × s̲in omega × t
  nxt ← damp × wall × ((2.0 × u) − p) + (c2 × ᵘl̲ap u) + drive
  (nxt, u, t + 1.0)
}
