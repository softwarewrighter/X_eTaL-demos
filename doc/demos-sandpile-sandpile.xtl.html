programdemos/sandpile/sandpile.xtl

The abelian sandpile: drop grains on a grid; a cell with 4 or more grains topples, giving one to each of its four neighbors, and grains that fall off the edge are lost. Repeat until every cell has fewer than 4. The order of the topples does not matter (that is what abelian means), so every cell topples at once, as often as it can: one array expression a round. The stable pile is a fractal. Run it with "xetal run demos/sandpile/sandpile.xtl"; it prints whether the pile is stable, the grains left, the total topples, and the pile drawn with characters. The live page runs it round by round, colored by grains, and lets you drop more: https://softwarewrighter.github.io/X_eTaL-demos/sandpile/
