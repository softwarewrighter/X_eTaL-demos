1⍝!/usr/bin/env xetal 2⍝# Reaction-diffusion (Gray-Scott): two chemicals U and V on a grid. 3⍝# U is fed in, V eats U to make more V (U + 2V -> 3V), V is removed, 4⍝# and both spread to their neighbors. Every step is a few array 5⍝# expressions over the whole grid: four shifted copies for the 6⍝# spreading, products for the reaction. The patterns (spots, mazes, 7⍝# coral) appear by themselves. Run it with "xetal run 8⍝# demos/reaction-diffusion/reaction-diffusion.xtl"; it prints the 9⍝# seeded grid's shape, a maze drawn with characters, and V's total 10⍝# (grown from the seed's 9.0). The live page runs it frame by frame 11⍝# and lets you click a cell for its neighbors and its update's 12⍝# arithmetic: https://softwarewrighter.github.io/X_eTaL-demos/reaction-diffusion/ 13 14⍝## The settings 15 16⍝# n: the grid's side. du, dv: how fast U and V spread. f, k: the feed 17⍝# and kill rates (0.029, 0.057 gives a maze; coral is 0.0545, 0.062, 18⍝# ripples 0.022, 0.051, spots 0.035, 0.065, worms 0.046, 0.063). 19⍝# steps: how many to run. 20n ← 32 21du ← 0.2 22dv ← 0.1 23f ← 0.029 24k ← 0.057 25steps ← 1200 26 27⍝# A matrix as one plane of a rank-3 array (u:p_lane), for stacking 28⍝# U and V with c_at. 29ᵘp̲lane ← { m → (1 c̲at s̲hape m) r̲eshape m } 30 31⍝## The Laplacian: four shifted copies minus four times the center 32 33⍝# u:l_ap x: how much a cell differs from its four neighbors, up down 34⍝# left right (o_-_1, o_-_2), added and the center subtracted four 35⍝# times over (the edges wrap). 36ᵘl̲ap ← { x → ((1 o̲-₁ x) + (-1 o̲-₁ x) + (1 o̲-₂ x) + -1 o̲-₂ x) − 4.0 × x } 37 38⍝## One step for every cell at once 39 40⍝# u:s_tep s: the state s is two planes, U and V. Each spreads by its 41⍝# own rate times its Laplacian; u * v * v is where V meets U and 42⍝# turns U into V (the reaction); U is fed at rate f, V removed at 43⍝# rate f + k. 44ᵘs̲tep ← { s → 45 u ← 1 s̲elect s 46 v ← 2 s̲elect s 47 uvv ← u × v × v 48 (ᵘp̲lane u + ((du × ᵘl̲ap u) − uvv) + f × 1.0 − u) c̲at ᵘp̲lane v + ((dv × ᵘl̲ap v) + uvv) − (f + k) × v 49} 50⍝ -- end of the core --------------------------------------------------- 51 52⍝# sq, seed, s0: the start, U everywhere, a square of V off the center. 53sq ← ((o̲ffsets n) ≥ 10) ∧ (o̲ffsets n) < 16 54seed ← f̲loat sq '∧ t̲able sq 55s0 ← (ᵘp̲lane 1.0 − 0.5 × seed) c̲at ᵘp̲lane 0.25 × seed 56s̲hape s0 57 58⍝# s, v: the state after all the steps (function power), and V's 59⍝# plane; drawn with a character per cell by how much V it has. 60s ← steps 'ᵘs̲tep p̲ower s0 61v ← 2 s̲elect s 62(1 + f̲loor 4.99 × (v m̲in 0.399) ÷ 0.4) s̲elect " .:o@" 63 64⍝ The total of V, rounded: the pattern has grown from the seed's 9.0. 65(f̲loor 0.5 + 100 × '+ r̲/ r̲avel v) ÷ 100
