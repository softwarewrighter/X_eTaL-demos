programdemos/nbody/nbody.xtl

N-body gravity with no loops. Every pair of bodies at once: the displacements of all pairs form a cube (2 x N x N, built by broadcasting with t_able), the cube gives every pair's softened inverse-square pull, and one reduce over the second body sums the pulls into each body's acceleration. A kick-drift-kick (leapfrog) step moves them all. Run it with "xetal run demos/nbody/nbody.xtl"; it prints the cube's shape, its x plane, the starting accelerations, the state after 50 steps, and the total momentum (which stays 0). The live page has four presets (the figure-eight, a binary star, a collapsing cluster, a Kepler ellipse) and lets you click a body for its row of the cube: https://softwarewrighter.github.io/X_eTaL-demos/nbody/
