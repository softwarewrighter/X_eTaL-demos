1⍝!/usr/bin/env xetal 2⍝# N-body gravity with no loops. Every pair of bodies at once: the 3⍝# displacements of all pairs form a cube (2 x N x N, built by 4⍝# broadcasting with t_able), the cube gives every pair's softened 5⍝# inverse-square pull, and one reduce over the second body sums the 6⍝# pulls into each body's acceleration. A kick-drift-kick (leapfrog) 7⍝# step moves them all. Run it with "xetal run demos/nbody/nbody.xtl"; 8⍝# it prints the cube's shape, its x plane, the starting 9⍝# accelerations, the state after 50 steps, and the total momentum 10⍝# (which stays 0). The live page has four presets (the figure-eight, 11⍝# a binary star, a collapsing cluster, a Kepler ellipse) and lets you 12⍝# click a body for its row of the cube: 13⍝# https://softwarewrighter.github.io/X_eTaL-demos/nbody/ 14 15⍝# g, eps2, dt, m, steps: G = 1, the softening (keeps close passes 16⍝# finite), the time step, the masses, and how many steps to run.
